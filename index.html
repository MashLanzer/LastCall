About

No face.
Only a voice.

LAST CALL is dark pop for the hours nobody talks about — minor-key hooks, red neon, and a presence that never shows its face.

The debut single, “Sleep Tight,” twists a lullaby into a warning: a voice close enough to comfort you, and just wrong enough to keep you awake.

It is only the first transmission. More are coming after dark.
