About

The Artist

Just the
music.

LAST CALL is a dark pop project built around minor-key hooks, red neon and modern production. The singer stays off camera — that's part of the concept. What you get is the music, front and center.

The second single is “Wide Awake,” released on November 27, 2026 — written by Brayan Ibarra and produced by Mashlanzer. The first release is the single “Sleep Tight,” released on November 10, 2026.

Two singles are out so far, and more music is in the works.
