About

The Artist

No face.
Only a voice.

LAST CALL is dark pop for the middle of the night — minor-key hooks, red light, and a singer you never actually see. That's the whole deal, and it's on purpose.

“Sleep Tight” takes the last words you hear before sleep and says them like a threat. It sings soft. It doesn't mean it soft.

One single so far. There's more coming — written at night, like this one.
