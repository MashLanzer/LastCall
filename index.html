The Artist

Just the

LAST CALL is a dark pop project built around minor-key hooks, red neon and modern production. The singer stays off camera — that's part of the concept. What you get is the music, front and center.

The first release is the single “Sleep Tight,” released on November 10, 2026 — written by Brayan Ibarra and produced by Mashlanzer.

One single is out so far, and more music is in the works.
